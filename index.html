⌨️ TypeStrike

· typing· speed· accuracy· multiplayer
A high-velocity typing arcade where you strike moving targets by typing their text before they escape the screen.

▶ Briefing

Typestrike is a mission-based typing game with 10 escalating stages. Targets — letters, numbers, symbols, or words — appear with various movement patterns: ricochet, falling, flying, zigzag, converging, spiraling, or chaotic. Your job: type the target's text before it leaves the play area or before time runs out. Each correct hit builds combo and score; missed targets and wrong keystrokes cost you. Complete all targets in a stage to advance.

▶ How to Play

  • Type the text of any visible target — the game automatically locks onto the most urgent target matching your input.
  • SPACE — detonate all active targets (costs 10s from the timer).
  • ENTER — start/continue through menus.
  • On touch devices, use the on-screen keyboard (toggle with the ⌨ button).
  • Each stage has a goal (number of targets to destroy) and a time budget. Complete the goal before time runs out.
  • Your combo resets on miss — maintain it for higher scores.
Accept Mission

⚡ Cell Jump

· tapping· reflex· speed· timing
A high-speed grid-based reflex test where you must strike the glowing cell before time expires.

▶ Briefing

Cell Jump is a pulse-pounding reaction game for 1–6 players. A single cell on a grid lights up with a glowing orb. Your mission: tap it before the timer hits zero. Each successful hit increases your streak and slightly reduces the next turn's time — pushing your reflexes to the limit. In multiplayer, players rotate turns, and the one with the highest streak at the end wins.

▶ How to Play

  • Set the grid size (columns × rows), time limit (0–5s), and number of players.
  • Click the glowing cell before the timer runs out.
  • Each correct hit: +1 streak, time shrinks slightly.
  • Each miss: streak resets, turn passes to next player.
  • In single-player: the game ends when you miss — your final streak is your score.
Take A Leap

🚗 Transporter

· driving· speed· precision· pursuit
A dynamic road-racing game where you navigate a procedurally generated scribbled route while evading police or chasing hostiles.

▶ Briefing

Trinity Drive drops you behind the wheel of a high-performance vehicle on a road that never stays the same. The path from point A to point Z is randomly generated each time — a winding, scribbled line through a 3D landscape. Choose your mode: Defensive (safe driving), Aggressive (chase fleeing hostiles), or Evasive (outrun police). The mini-map shows your position, enemies, and the road ahead.

▶ How to Play

  • WASD or Arrow Keys — drive (up/down/left/right).
  • SPACE — auto-stop / brake.
  • ENTER — speed boost (×2).
  • V — cycle through camera views (First, Rear, Third, Drone).
  • P — pause the game.
  • Follow the road from A (green marker) to Z (red marker). Reaching Z regenerates a new road and awards points.
  • Use the mini-map (draggable) to plan your route and track enemies.

🏗️ Citibuild

· crane· precision· accuracy· control
Operate a construction crane to place blocks with millimeter precision — wind and time are against you.

▶ Briefing

Citibuild puts you in the cab of a tower crane at the Riverreach Bay reconstruction site. Your task: pick up blocks from the spawn pile and lower them into target slots. The wind sways your load, and the clock is ticking. Each perfect placement earns gold; each miss reduces structural integrity. Complete all slots to finish the stage and unlock the next chapter of the harbor's revival.

▶ How to Play

  • WASD or Arrow Keys — move the trolley horizontally.
  • Q / E or vertical pad — raise/lower the hook.
  • SPACE or GRAB button — pick up a block from the spawn pile.
  • Move the block over a ghost slot (it turns green when aligned).
  • Press SPACE or GRAB again to drop the block.
  • Place all blocks to complete the stage. Misses reduce your integrity — if it hits zero, the structure collapses.

✈️ APEX WING

· flight sim· 3D graphics· combat· web audio
A high-octane 3D flight trainer and combat simulator featuring realistic aerodynamics, dynamic dogfights, and multiple aircraft.

▶ Briefing

Apex Wing is an advanced WebGL flight simulator and combat trainer. Choose between the agile F-9 Razorwing Jet or the precision H-4 Kestrel Helicopter to navigate training courses, execute low-altitude coastal maneuvers, and engage enemy ground armor, naval vessels, and air squadrons across multiple dynamic environments.

▶ How to Play

  • W / S — control engine throttle and speed.
  • Mouse / Arrow Keys — adjust pitch (nose up/down) and bank roll for coordinated turns.
  • A / D or Q / E — rudder yaw and precision directional control.
  • SPACE or Left Click — fire homing missiles / primary cannons.
  • P / M — pause simulation / toggle synthesized WebAudio score.
  • Switch dynamically between Cockpit, External Chase, and Drone Overview camera angles during flight.

The Ecosystem

To understand ForraNova, you first have to understand ForraCorp — because ForraNova doesn't exist independently of it. ForraCorp is the parent entity: the umbrella corporation through which Mr. Ubi Fredrick (also known as Ethan Audrick) channels a career that has moved from hardware engineering, through electronics and telecommunications, into machine learning and AI strategy. ForraNova is one of ForraCorp's core sectors — specifically, its instructional and educational operating wing.


Think of it this way: ForraCorp is the practice. ForraNova is the classroom.

The Entity: ForraCorp

Resilience in innovation, Precision in design, Durability in service.


ForraCorp is where the actual building happens. It's the commercial and creative vehicle behind every delivered project — whether that project is a prompt-engineered AI workflow, a Python automation script, a resilient power infrastructure build, or a long-form cinematic literary work. As of this recording, ForraCorp's Projects Gallery holds more than 37 accomplished projects and counting, spanning categories that reflect its founder's genuinely unusual range: hardware precision, telecommunications engineering, AI systems, and creative storytelling all under one roof.


ForraCorp's guiding slogan captures its operating philosophy in three words: Resilience, Precision, Durability — in full, "Resilience in innovation and precision in design yield durability in service." It's a statement about how things are built, not just what gets built.


Critically, ForraCorp is a single-architect entity. There's no diffusion of vision across a large team — Ubi Fredrick is the Chief Architect and Principal Engineer behind every project that carries the ForraCorp name. That consistency of hand is part of what the brand is selling: you know who built it, and you know the standard it was built to.

The Sector: ForraNova

Knowledge is for the Living.


ForraNova is where the legacy gets taught. If ForraCorp is about building, ForraNova is about transferring what was learned while building. It's the sector where Ubi Fredrick distils what he calls his "train the trainer" legacy — years of hands-on technical and creative practice, repackaged into structured learning.


ForraNova operates as ForraCorp's primary instructional wing, structuring and delivering:

  • Standard computational competencies.
  • Linguistic engineering insight (a natural extension of Fredrick's own published prompting framework).
  • Algorithmic workflow training, etc.

It functions as a digital university of AI and modern technology, complete with enrollable courses, professional certification, and portfolio-building — plus a media arm in the form of podcasts, debates, and analysis on AI, most notably The AI Chat Show itself, hosted by Ubi Fredrick.


ForraNova's slogan isn't decorative. It's the operating thesis of the entire sector: knowledge that isn't transferred and used is knowledge wasted. Where ForraCorp proves competence through finished projects, ForraNova exists to make sure that competence doesn't stay locked inside one person.
